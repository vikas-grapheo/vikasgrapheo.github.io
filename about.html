OUR STORY

Turning Ideas Into Visual Identity.

Vikas Grapheo is an independent creative design studio built around the power of visual storytelling.

We believe design is more than just colours, shapes and typography. It is a way to communicate ideas, express identities and create memorable experiences.

From logos and branding to posters and digital graphics, our focus is on creating designs that communicate clearly and leave a lasting impression.
